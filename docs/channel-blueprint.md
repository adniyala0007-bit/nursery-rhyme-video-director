# Channel Blueprint

## Working identity

- **Working name:** Milo & Friends Nursery Rhymes
- **Short display name:** Milo & Friends
- **Status:** working title; confirm before making channel artwork
- **Audience:** preschool children and their families
- **Language:** English is the starter default; change per episode if needed
- **Promise:** gentle, catchy original songs that help little viewers sing, move, count, notice, and imagine

## Reference observations

The two supplied YouTube screenshots suggest a useful channel structure:

- A wide banner with a sunny, storybook setting and the main cast.
- A simple avatar that remains readable at small size.
- Bright, expressive 3D characters and a clear focal point in each thumbnail.
- Separate rows or playlists for full songs, learning collections, and Shorts.
- Repeated colors and character designs that make episodes feel like one channel.

These are presentation observations only. Make an original logo, character silhouettes, wording, palette, and thumbnail compositions. Do not use the supplied channel names, characters, screenshots, or distinctive artwork.

## Suggested channel layout

1. **Featured video:** a short welcome or newest full song.
2. **Start Here · Milo & Friends:** the most welcoming episodes for new viewers.
3. **Sing-Along Songs:** complete original rhymes.
4. **Learn with Milo:** gentle counting, colors, shapes, letters, and routines.
5. **Move and Dance:** simple, safe actions children can follow with a caregiver.
6. **Milo & Friends Shorts:** vertical excerpts that point viewers to a full episode.

## Artwork direction

- **Banner:** the approved Peach Stone Garden Lane, with the cast grouped to one side and ample clear space for the channel name. Keep important faces and text inside the YouTube channel-art safe area.
- **Avatar:** a close-up of one original mascot or a simple custom mark; strong outline and few details so it reads at small sizes.
- **Thumbnails:** one action, one emotion, a small cast cluster, and a few large props. Use short original text only when it helps comprehension.
- **Palette:** garden green, warm peach, soft cream, sky blue, and restrained coral accents. Maintain readable contrast.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Example topics | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sing | Hello, bedtime, weather, animals, friendship | 1–2 minute songs |
| Learn | Counting, colors, shapes, opposites, routines | 1–3 minute musical stories |
| Move | Clap, stretch, tiptoe, freeze, gentle dance | 15–30 second Shorts and full songs |
| Story | Small problem, kind choice, joyful resolution | 1–3 minute musical adventures |

## Publishing rhythm starter

Start with one carefully reviewed full episode per week and 2–3 Shorts cut from approved footage. Adjust after reviewing audience retention, repeat viewing, and production time. A steady, manageable schedule is more useful than a calendar that forces rushed work.

Replace the working name and description after confirming the final channel identity. Do not imply educational, developmental, or safety outcomes that a video does not support.
