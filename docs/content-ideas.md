# Starter Content Ideas

These are original theme starters, not finished songs. Build one tiny story and one repeatable chorus around each.

1. Good Morning, Garden Friends — greeting and noticing the morning.
2. The Little Rain Dance — weather and gentle movement.
3. Count the Peach Stones — counting along the garden path.
4. Pip Finds the Blue Flower — colors and helping a friend.
5. Bubo's Bedtime Stars — a calm bedtime wind-down.
6. The Tippy-Toe Garden Walk — quiet/loud movement contrast.
7. Lila's Shape Hunt — circles, squares, and triangles in the garden.
8. Milo's Sharing Song — taking turns with a ball or toy.
9. The Friendly Wind — noticing leaves and clouds.
10. One, Two, Three, Clap with Me — movement and counting.
11. The Lost Little Button — noticing details and asking a grown-up for help.
12. Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy Day — simple weather vocabulary.
13. The Garden Cleanup Song — putting toys away together.
14. Pip's Quiet Listening Game — listen for gentle garden sounds.
15. A Hello for Every Friend — greetings and inclusion.

Before production, search the planned title and lyric phrases to avoid confusingly similar songs or titles. Keep the song and melody original.
