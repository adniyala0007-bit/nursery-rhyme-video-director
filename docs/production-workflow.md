# Production Workflow

## Director Mode · scene-by-scene

1. **Brief:** choose one preschool-friendly theme and one simple learning or emotional idea.
2. **Treatment:** define the opening hook, tiny story, repeatable chorus, and happy ending.
3. **Lyrics:** write original, easy-to-sing words. Check that the rhyme and melody are not copied from a known song.
4. **Timing map:** fit every lyric phrase to a timestamp and planned action.
5. **Storyboard:** use a small number of clear shots; maintain the same set, lighting, and character designs.
6. **Reference approval:** confirm character sheets, palette, environment, and voice style before generating the full video.
7. **Test clip:** make a 5–10 second sample and check faces, movement, sound, and captions.
8. **Production:** create approved shots and audio; revise only the sections that fail review.
9. **Edit:** assemble to the final vocal timing, add readable captions, music, and gentle sound design.
10. **Review:** watch the complete episode with sound and muted; check pacing, captions, safety, and licensing.

## Production Pack Mode · handoff bundle

Create all Director Mode outputs, then add:

- Thumbnail concept plus a clean no-text prompt.
- Three title options, a concise description, and a small set of relevant tags.
- A playlist destination and a suggested pinned-comment draft for a parent-facing channel.
- One or two vertical Shorts cutdowns with a complete beginning and ending.
- Credits and license notes for every external asset, voice, and music track.

## Two-minute timing guide

Use approximately 8–12 shots for a two-minute song. Each shot may last 8–15 seconds, with longer holds for a chorus that children can repeat. The approved voice track is the timing source of truth; do not force footage to a fixed number of seconds if it makes the song feel rushed.

## Review gates

| Gate | Review question | Proceed when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treatment | Is the theme simple and kind? | Story fits the target age and runtime |
| Lyrics | Are the words original and singable? | No borrowed lyrics or unclear phrases |
| Style test | Are characters and setting on-model? | Reference look is consistent |
| Audio test | Is the voice/music comfortable and clear? | No clipping, harsh peaks, or distracting effects |
| Final cut | Is every action safe and easy to understand? | Captions, ending, rights, and audience settings are reviewed |

## Child-friendly production defaults

- Prefer gentle stakes and reassuring resolutions.
- Show safe behavior and adult help where a real-world activity could be risky.
- Do not ask children for personal information, direct messages, purchases, or off-platform contact.
- Avoid flashing edits, startling audio, humiliating jokes, and unsafe imitation.
- Do not add spoken calls to action aimed at children to share personal details or follow external links.
