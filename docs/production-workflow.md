# Production Workflow

## Full nursery-rhyme default

Every full episode is **16:9 landscape** and runs **1:30–2:00 (90–120 seconds)**. Keep Shorts separate in 9:16. Use an approved voice/music timing map as the final timing source; do not compress lyrics to fit a fixed number of shots.

## Director Mode · scene-by-scene

1. **Brief:** choose one preschool-friendly theme and one simple learning or emotional idea.
2. **Treatment:** define the opening hook, tiny story, repeatable chorus, and reassuring ending.
3. **Lyrics:** write original, easy-to-sing verses. Include character names naturally when they appear, and mark every repeated chorus.
4. **Timing map:** timestamp all verses and instrumental breaks; confirm the total song is 90–120 seconds.
5. **Storyboard:** plan about 8–12 clear shots. Match each lyric phrase to a named character and understandable action.
6. **Reference approval:** attach Milo, Lila, Pip, or Bubo's approved reference sheet to each shot where that character appears.
7. **Test clip:** make a 5–10 second sample and check faces, outfits, movement, sound, and framing.
8. **Production:** create the approved shots in landscape 16:9; revise only sections that fail review.
9. **Edit:** assemble the clips to the voice track; add readable captions, original music, and gentle sound design.
10. **Review:** watch the full episode with sound and muted; check runtime, captions, safety, continuity, and licensing.

## Copy-ready prompt requirements

Every full-video prompt must say:

- Complete video duration: **90–120 seconds**.
- Canvas: **16:9 landscape**.
- Named cast from the character bible and the active names for each shot.
- The timed original verses and chorus that the visuals follow.
- Shot-level duration, action, composition, camera movement, and sound cue.
- Use attached character sheets; do not change names, faces, palette, fixed outfits, or proportions.
- No generated lettering; add captions in the editor.

If a storyboard uses separate clips, ensure the clip durations add to the requested 90–120 second full episode.

## Production Pack Mode · handoff bundle

Create all Director Mode outputs, then add:

- Thumbnail concept plus a clean no-text prompt.
- Three title options, a concise description, and a small set of relevant tags.
- A playlist destination and a suggested parent-facing pinned-comment draft.
- One or two vertical Shorts cutdowns with a complete beginning and ending.
- Credits and license notes for every external asset, voice, and music track.

## Review gates

| Gate | Review question | Proceed when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treatment | Is the theme simple and kind? | Story fits the target age and 90–120 second runtime |
| Lyrics | Are the words original and singable? | No borrowed lyrics or unclear phrases |
| Character test | Are named characters on-model? | The correct references are attached and the sheet details match |
| Audio test | Is the voice/music comfortable and clear? | No clipping, harsh peaks, or distracting effects |
| Final cut | Is every action safe and easy to understand? | Runtime, captions, rights, and audience settings are reviewed |

## Child-friendly production defaults

- Prefer gentle stakes and reassuring resolutions.
- Show safe behavior and adult help where a real-world activity could be risky.
- Do not ask children for personal information, direct messages, purchases, or off-platform contact.
- Avoid flashing edits, startling audio, humiliating jokes, and unsafe imitation.
- Do not add spoken calls to action aimed at children to share personal details or follow external links.
