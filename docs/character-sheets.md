## Master character-sheet prompt

Copy this prompt when a turnaround needs to be recreated or refined. Attach the current cast lineup and the named character's existing turnaround as references.

```text
Create a polished 3D preschool nursery-rhyme character turnaround for [EXACT CHARACTER NAME].
Use the attached Milo & Friends cast lineup and the existing [EXACT CHARACTER NAME] turnaround as the identity references. Show that same original character in front, side, and back views, full body, on a clean warm-cream background with soft studio lighting. Preserve the exact face, silhouette, eye color, hair/fur, outfit, fixed accessories, colors, and childlike proportions shown in the reference. Keep the soft rounded 3D nursery-rhyme animation finish. Do not add other characters, props, text, logos, watermarks, or source-channel designs. Change only the detail specifically requested.
```

## Character-specific prompts

### Milo

> Make a three-view model sheet for **Milo**: cheerful preschool boy with warm peach skin, short wavy chestnut hair swept to one side, large teal eyes, turquoise striped T-shirt, sunny-yellow short overalls, and white sneakers. Keep his face, hair silhouette, outfit, colors, and proportions identical across front, side, and back views.

### Lila

> Make a three-view model sheet for **Lila**: kind preschool girl with warm medium-brown skin, dark curly hair in two round puffs with lilac bows, large brown eyes, coral-pink short-sleeved shirt, purple/lavender pinafore with small flower appliqués, and yellow shoes. Keep the curls, bows, face, outfit, colors, and proportions identical across all views.

### Pip

> Make a three-view model sheet for **Pip**, a small plush-like mint-blue bunny with one short upright ear and one softly floppy ear, pink inner ears, cream muzzle and belly, large teal-blue eyes, a small coral nose, and coral feet. Pip wears no clothes or accessories. Keep the asymmetric ears, face, colors, and proportions identical across views.

### Bubo

> Make a three-view model sheet for **Bubo**, a friendly tangerine-orange monkey with a cream face and belly, large brown eyes, rounded ears, a small orange hair tuft, and a tail with a curled tip. Bubo always wears a teal sleeveless vest with two yellow buttons and no shoes. Keep his markings, tail, vest, colors, and proportions identical across views.

## Reuse rules

1. Attach the matching turnaround PNG to every shot where **Milo**, **Lila**, **Pip**, or **Bubo** appears.
2. Attach [cast-lineup.png](../assets/character-sheets/cast-lineup.png) as an optional additional style reference.
3. Name each active character explicitly in the verse and every matching video prompt.
4. Keep fixed designs the same across the whole song and across future episodes.
5. If the owner changes a design, update the corresponding PNG, this prompt guide, and the character bible together.
