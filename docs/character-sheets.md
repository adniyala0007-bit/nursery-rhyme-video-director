## Master character-sheet creation prompt

Copy this prompt into an image generator when creating a new model sheet. Generate one named character sheet at a time and attach the current SVG as the reference. Do not accept a changed design without updating the bible and all sheet files.

```text
Create a clean, readable character model sheet for [EXACT CHARACTER NAME] from the original “Milo & Friends Nursery Rhymes” preschool storybook world. Use the attached existing character sheet as the primary visual reference. Show the same character in front, side, and back views with the exact same age/species, silhouette, face, colors, fixed clothing/accessories, and proportions in all views. Add a small expression row with happy, curious, and calm expressions. Use a warm, softly lit 3D storybook finish, simple cream background, clear separation between poses, and a small labeled palette of the approved colors. Keep the full character visible, with neutral relaxed pose and no props. Do not add text except the exact name “[EXACT CHARACTER NAME]” and the view labels. Do not redesign, add accessories, change the outfit, introduce other characters, or copy any existing channel artwork.
```

## Character-specific prompt details

### Milo

> Make a three-view model sheet for **Milo**: preschool-age boy with warm light-peach skin, a round face, dark teal eyes, and short chestnut hair with one curved forelock. He always wears a mustard-yellow hoodie with a teal front pocket, deep-indigo shorts, and teal sneakers with cream soles. Show front, side, and back views plus happy, curious, and calm expressions. Keep his face, hairline, colors, outfit, and child proportions identical in every view.

### Lila

> Make a three-view model sheet for **Lila**: preschool-age girl with warm light-peach skin, hazel-brown eyes, and chestnut hair in two rounded buns with small coral clips. She always wears a cream long-sleeve shirt, a lavender overall dress, and coral shoes. Show front, side, and back views plus happy, curious, and calm expressions. Keep both buns, clips, face, colors, outfit, and child proportions identical in every view.

### Pip

> Make a three-view model sheet for **Pip**, a small rounded turquoise-blue songbird with a lemon-cream belly, a dark-teal feather tuft, large dark eyes, a tangerine beak, and tangerine feet. Pip wears no clothes or accessories. Show front, side, and back views plus happy, curious, and calm expressions; use small grounded poses. Preserve the same beak, feather colors, wing shape, scale, and proportions.

### Bubo

> Make a three-view model sheet for **Bubo**, a rounded cocoa-brown owl with cream face discs and belly, amber brow tufts, and large dark-brown eyes. Bubo always wears the same teal scarf tied at the side. Show front, side, and back views plus kind, curious, and calm expressions. Preserve the same facial markings, body shape, scarf, colors, and proportions in every view.

## Reuse rules

1. Keep one approved model version per character and store the reference filenames in the episode brief.
2. Attach the correct sheet to every prompt where **Milo**, **Lila**, **Pip**, or **Bubo** appears.
3. Name the active characters explicitly in each video prompt and each corresponding verse.
4. Do not describe the same character with changing synonyms that could create a new design.
5. If a design changes, update the SVG, this prompt guide, and the character bible together before making the next episode.
