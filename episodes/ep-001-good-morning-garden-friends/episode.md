# Good Morning, Garden Friends

- **ID:** ep-001
- **Status:** Sample production pack
- **Format:** 16:9 full episode
- **Target runtime:** 2:00
- **Language:** English
- **Theme:** Welcoming the morning in the garden
- **Learning / feeling goal:** Sing a friendly greeting, notice colors, and count from one to three
- **Cast:** Milo, Lila, Pip, Bubo
- **Location:** Peach Stone Garden Lane
- **Music reference:** Original, gentle 104 BPM acoustic pop; ukulele, soft hand percussion, warm bass, light bell accents. Do not imitate an existing song.

## Creative treatment

The camera wakes with the garden. Milo and Lila greet the morning, Pip flutters to a peach-colored stone, and Bubo invites everyone to notice three bright flowers. A repeatable “hello” chorus brings the friends together. The ending slows down to a wave and a calm goodbye. Keep the action simple, friendly, and safe.
